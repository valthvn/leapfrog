Leapfrog lets you play on 26.3 servers like DonutSMP with a 1.21.11 Fabric client, and actually see the new stuff.

Normally, when an older client joins a 26.3 server through ViaBackwards, everything new gets swapped for something old. Cushions turn into floating carpets, wool stairs turn into quartz stairs, sulfur cubes turn into slimes. Leapfrog puts the real content back on your screen.

## What it restores

- Cushions, with their 3D model, colour, sitting height and sounds
- 115 blocks from 26.x (wool and concrete stairs and slabs, poplar, sulfur, cinnabar, potent sulfur, golden dandelion, straw bed...) with the right shapes, light and sounds
- 153 items with their real model, texture and name
- The new baby mobs (48 models: cow, pig, chicken, sheep, cat, wolf, fox, panda, horse, villager...)
- Sulfur cubes, translucent with their core
- Geysers on potent sulfur: bubbles, gas, the eruption and the push up
- 151 new sounds
- The redrawn 26.x textures (you can turn them off)

Every feature can be turned on or off in the settings (K key, or through Mod Menu).

## Requirements

Fabric API, ViaFabric, ViaVersion and ViaBackwards. Keep ViaVersion and ViaBackwards on the same version.

On DonutSMP (donutsmp.net and its subdomains), your client joins as 26.3 automatically. You can add other servers in the settings. Everywhere else ViaFabric stays off, so nothing changes there.

## First launch

The mod doesn't include any Mojang files. The first time you start the game, it downloads the official 26.3 client from Mojang, keeps the textures, models, sounds and names it needs (about 12 MB, in `.minecraft/leapfrog/26.3/`) and deletes the rest. It takes a few seconds on the loading screen, once. If you're offline it just tries again next time.

## Is it allowed?

It only changes what you see and hear. No hidden info, and nothing is sent that a normal client wouldn't send. The one thing that touches movement is the geyser push, which works the same as on a real 26.3 client. Safe mode turns it off. If you're unsure, ask your server's staff.
